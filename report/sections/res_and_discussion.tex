\section{Results and Discussion}
\label{sec:results}
The performance of the implemented algorithms was evaluated through a series of simulation experiments in Gazebo and with the turtlebot3 in laboratory, focusing on the robot's ability to track a dynamic goal while avoiding obstacles. The results are presented and discussed for each task as follows. The performance metrics defined in Section~\ref{ssec:metrics} were computed and analyzed to assess the effectiveness of the control strategies.
